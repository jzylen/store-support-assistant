Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a credit card to start?

▼
No. Your 7-day free trial starts immediately with no credit card required. You only need to add payment details when you decide to upgrade.

What happens when I hit my message limit?

▼
Your assistant will let customers know it's temporarily unavailable and direct them to your support contact. You can upgrade your plan at any time to restore full access. Message counts reset at the start of every month.

Can I cancel anytime?

▼
Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel or change your plan at any time from your dashboard with no penalty.
