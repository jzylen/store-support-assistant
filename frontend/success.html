How to add it to your website

  1. Log in to your Shopify Admin
  2. Go to Online Store → Themes
  3. Click Actions → Edit Code on your active theme
  4. In the left sidebar open Layout → theme.liquid
  5. Find the closing </body> tag near the bottom
  6. Paste your embed code directly above it
  7. Click Save
💡 The widget will now appear on every page of your Shopify store.
